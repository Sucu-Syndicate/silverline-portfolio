Palette directions

design spec · v0.3

The current parchment + green register reads environmental annual report, not developer portfolio. Below are three directions that keep your forest green and OKLCH warm-tinted neutrals but shift the dominant surface so the site reads dev-coded immediately. Each has a mini hero preview in its actual colors, full token table, type system, and pros/cons. The recommended option is flagged at the bottom of each card.

01 — Direction

Three palettes, same green

Each preview shows the same hero composition rendered in its native palette. The green stays consistent — what shifts is the surface and contrast register.

Recommended

05 — Trade-offs

Pick by what you want first

A · Plotter

Hero terminal lives in its native habitat — no register fight
Reads dev-coded in 1 second to anyone scanning
Green has the highest contrast and impact here
Demolition easter egg lands hardest on dark
Less differentiated from other dev portfolios at first glance
Long-form reading needs a second register on /blog

B · Workshop

Warmer + more lived-in than A
Reads as one register everywhere — simplest system
Best for "thoughtful builder" angle
Less crisp than A — green pops less
Risks reading hobbyist if executed soft

C · Spec sheet

Most editorial — closest to a design publication
Strongest "designs systems, not just ships features" read
Long-form reading already at home
Hero terminal becomes inverted block — loses some cool factor
Heaviest lift to keep from looking like every Vercel landing page
Cursor trail and grain effects are weaker on light
My recommendation

Direction A · Plotter

The site's central concept is the hero terminal — a live worklog as the headline. That concept needs to live on a dark surface to read correctly, and dropping the register switch means the worklog hero never has to argue with parchment. Direction B is the safe second choice if A reads too cold once built. C is interesting but is fighting the worklog concept, not amplifying it. Ship A. Bring back warm cream as the /blog body register only — that's where it earns its place.
